# Post

Rule 0: blunt. Say if the idea is weak or the claim unsupported. No hype words, no fake numbers.
Inputs: the idea, `PRODUCT.md` (ICP, pain language, tone), `GROWTH.md` (pillars), any real metric or proof.
Platform rules come from `WOLF/GROW.md`; re-read it, it changes.

**Shape for every cut:** hook (a specific pain, a surprising number, or a strong claim) → proof (a real example, metric, or before/after) → one takeaway → CTA (one action).

- **X:** ≤280 characters, or a thread of ≤5 posts. Written to earn replies and DM shares: end on a question or a bold, defensible claim. Native image or video when possible. A link is allowed with context. No hashtags beyond one.
- **LinkedIn:** a 1–2 line hook before "see more", short paragraphs, one topic only (stay on the account's theme), 120–250 words. The link goes in the first comment. End with a question that invites real comments, never engagement bait.
- **Substack Note:** 1–3 sentences with a sharp opinion and a link to the essay. **Essay outline:** title, 5 section heads, the one story, the one metric.

**Output:** for each platform, 1 main draft + 2 variants (different hooks), a suggested visual (hand off to `ad`), and the best posting slot as a guess labelled UNVERIFIED.
Never post, DM, or email on the user's behalf. Publishing is the human's action.
